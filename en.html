About

MUSIC, EXPERIENCE AND A FEELING FOR THE MOMENT.

I’m DJ KEGGY, based in Poreč, Istria. For over 15 years, I have provided music for weddings, clubs, private celebrations and events.

Today, my focus is on weddings in Istria, throughout Croatia, Germany and Europe. After many years in Switzerland, where I provided music for numerous weddings, I now perform at local and international weddings across Europe. With experience from around 40 weddings per year, I bring together different cultures and generations through music.

From Balkan and Croatian music to House, Afro House, 90s, 2000s, Pop, Rock, Hip-Hop, R&B, Latin, Reggaeton and international party hits, every music selection is personal.

No two weddings are the same, so there is no standard playlist.

My aim goes beyond playing music. I want to play the right song at the right moment and create an atmosphere that the couple and their guests will remember for years.
